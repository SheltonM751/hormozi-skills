# DEMO 1: Grand Slam Offer Builder

Copy-paste this into Claude Code while inside the hormozi-claude-code-brain repo.

## The Prompt

```
Read the following playbooks before responding:
- playbooks/pricing.md
- playbooks/fast-cash.md

Build me a Grand Slam Offer for my business:

**My business:** Online fitness coaching for busy professionals (30-50 year old men)
**My target customer:** High-income professionals who want to lose 20-40 lbs but don't have time for the gym
**Current price point:** $200/month
**Main result you deliver:** 20 lbs lost in 90 days with 3x/week 30-minute home workouts

Using Hormozi's Value Equation (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood / Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice), create:

1. The Dream Outcome statement
2. 3-5 ways to increase Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
3. 3-5 ways to decrease Time Delay
4. 3-5 ways to decrease Effort & Sacrifice
5. Bonuses that make the offer feel like a "stupid deal"
6. The recommended price point with justification
7. A guarantee structure
8. The one-sentence offer statement for ads and sales calls
```
